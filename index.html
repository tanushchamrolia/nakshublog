The Balance Sheet

The Balance Sheet is my space to talk about finance in our day-to-day lives — informative but chill, honest, and expressive. It's where I break down how I think about the world, money, and the way finance shows up around us. The whole look is warm and calm, with brown coffee colors, white, beige, and cream. My logo is a coffee cup with a ladder leaning on it and a person climbing up — a little symbol of growth, learning, and leveling up. This blog is my way of sharing my thoughts, my perspective, and my approach to money in a real, grounded way.
